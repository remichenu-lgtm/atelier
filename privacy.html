Privacy Policy

Atelier · Last updated 11 November 2026

About this app

Atelier is a personal app for Mac and iPhone, built and used only by its developer (remi). It is not distributed to other people.

Data the app accesses

With the developer's own authorization, Atelier reads the developer's own Pinterest boards and Pins through the Pinterest API, read-only, to display them in the app. It never creates, edits or deletes anything on Pinterest and never accesses other users' data.

How data is stored

  • Pin images are kept only on the developer's own devices, so they can be viewed offline.
  • No Pin titles, descriptions, links or Pinterest identifiers are stored.
  • The Pinterest access token is stored in the device's secure Keychain.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared, published or sent to any third party. The app has no ads, analytics or tracking, and no server of its own.

Retention and deletion

Disconnecting Pinterest in the app deletes the access token. Deleting a board, or the app, deletes its stored images. If access is revoked or Pinterest requests it, all stored Pinterest data is deleted within 30 days.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through this project's GitHub page.
