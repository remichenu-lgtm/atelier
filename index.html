Atelier

A personal visual workspace for Mac and iPhone.

What it is

Atelier is an infinite whiteboard (sticky notes, shapes, arrows, drawing and images) with image boards for collecting inspiration. Everything is stored on the device, so it works offline.

Pinterest

Atelier can show the developer's own Pinterest boards, read-only, inside the app. It only reads the boards of the signed-in account, never writes to Pinterest, and keeps only the images, on the developer's own devices.

Who uses it

Atelier is a personal project, built and used by a single person (remi). It is not distributed or sold.
